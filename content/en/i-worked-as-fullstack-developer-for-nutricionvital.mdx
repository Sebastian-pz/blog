I worked as a fullstack developer on the Nutrición Vital shop, an Argentine company that sells supplements and meal plans. The team was several fullstack developers.

They sold through gyms and wanted customers to buy from home and receive the order. The shop was built from scratch: catalog, account, and payment.

![Nutrición Vital shop showing the product catalog](/blog/webVitalexample.gif)

> The GIF is compressed so it weighs less. It loses sharpness.

My part was the code for those flows. I also put folders and conventions in order where the code was getting mixed together. I did not redesign the architecture.

On the server: documents in MongoDB with Mongoose, an Express API, and authentication with JWT. Authorization separated what a user could read from what they could change. Checkout went through Mercado Pago. The order notice went through a mail service.

On the client: React, Redux, and Bootstrap.

![Nutrición Vital checkout using Mercado Pago](/blog/webVitalPayExample.gif)

Stack I worked with: TypeScript, Node.js, Express, React, Redux, Bootstrap, JWT, Mercado Pago, MongoDB with Mongoose, and the mail service.

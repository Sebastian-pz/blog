A friend called me about a short job at a startup. They needed a chatbot, and I had never built one. The engagement left the implementation up to me.

I wrote it in Python, with two libraries. The first handled the message: it compared the text with the training examples and returned a reply. The second exposed that component so someone could actually talk to it.

Training stayed narrow. I fed it the company's own text, and the model answered customer questions from that material. It did not query a database or any other system. The reply came from the example set.

The bot stopped there. The material was small, and plenty of questions fell outside its coverage. The step I did not reach was connecting it to a database, so a reply could come from a query rather than from the trained text alone.

I built the web app for a bar to record sales, costs, expenses, and inventory. Before that, there was a temporary workbook, described in [Sales, inventory, and payroll for a bar, automated with VBA](/en/post/i-created-sales-inventory-and-payroll-system-using-visual-basic-for-apps).

In November a mutual contact asked me to look at how the place was run. Someone else had already been hired to build sales software, so my part was an Excel workbook for sales and inventory until that software arrived. In late November I got the rest of the context.

The bar wanted its own system. What they were about to receive was a license for an existing program, plus a high monthly fee, and running it required Windows. The shop uses a MacBook Pro. The owner rejected that delivery.

I started the app the following Monday. The scope was the shop's: sales, costs, expenses, and inventory, served in the browser.

Stack: TypeScript, React, Vite, Tailwind, Next.js, Node.js, Express, NestJS, MongoDB, and PostgreSQL.

A bar had no information system: no inventory, no payroll, no accounting books, and no supplier records.

The place had already paid for other software, so my deliverable was temporary. I built an Excel workbook for sales, inventory, and payroll until that software arrived.

Three procedures ended up in VBA:

- compute the total for each table
- decrement inventory when a sale is recorded
- record the inflow as cash or as an increase in accounts receivable

Part of the work was accounting. The accounts had to be named, not just moved between cells.

Once the workbook was in use I saw that this place often splits a table's bill. I added the split payment, even though it was not in the original request.

With that, they could assemble the basic records needed to answer the DIAN (Colombia's tax authority) and the industry-and-commerce tax. It does not close the business's books. It leaves the record that did not exist.

The build was Excel and VBA macros. There was little branching: the procedure follows how the place operates. Excel can also be automated with Python. I did not use that here.

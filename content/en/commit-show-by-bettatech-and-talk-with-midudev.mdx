I have followed Midudev's work for years. On BettaTech's The Commit Show he goes through his experience and how he sees development work.

The part I kept is about first jobs. His criterion: a team that mentors you matters more than the starting salary. Mentoring, in his wording, is a person who reviews the work and stays with the decisions. He puts it this way: at the beginning it weighs more than pay.

Without that review, people learn to solve things alone. It can also become a load. Technical decisions have no second pair of eyes, and the person still learning pays the cost.

The talk goes on to salaries and to what counts as seniority. It is worth watching for that distinction.
